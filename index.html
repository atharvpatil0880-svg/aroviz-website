What We Do

Four disciplines, one accountable partner

PCBA Manufacturing

PCBA (Printed Circuit Board Assembly) manufacturing is a crucial step in producing electronic devices. It involves assembling electronic components onto a printed circuit board to create a functional electronic system.

It combines automated machinery, skilled labor, and quality control to produce reliable assemblies. Outsourcing to specialized facilities is often more cost-effective and efficient, especially for high-volume runs.

Engineering Services

A broad range of activities that support industries and projects.

Consulting Services: expert advice on technical aspects of a project, including feasibility studies, risk assessment, cost estimation, and project management.

Design and Development: mechanical design, electrical design, software development, and architectural design.

Cable and Harness

Organized sets of wires and cables that transmit electrical signals or power within electronic devices and systems.

Design: detailed schematics and drawings covering signal integrity, voltage requirements, space constraints, and environmental conditions.

Component Selection: cables, wires, connectors and terminals chosen for electrical specifications, mechanical durability, and environmental resistance.

Box Assembly

Assembling components into an enclosure to create a complete product, common across electronics, manufacturing, and consumer goods.

Component Preparation: testing components, attaching connectors or fasteners, and applying protective coatings.

Box Preparation: cleaning the enclosure, applying finishes, and installing hinges, latches, or mounting brackets.
